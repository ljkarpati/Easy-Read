      About

      EasyRead makes reading more comfortable. It shows any text in a calm, easy-to-read layout and can read it aloud, lighting up each sentence as it goes. It is free, has no ads and works without internet after your first visit.

      Privacy

      Your text never leaves this device. Scanning a page and opening files also happen on this device. There are no accounts and no tracking.

      Look up is the one exception: it sends only your search words to Wikipedia.

      Better voices

      On iPhone and iPad, open Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices. Pick your language and download a voice marked Enhanced or Premium. It is free.

      On a Mac, open System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content, then System voice → Manage Voices.

      Then choose the new voice under Aa → Voice.
